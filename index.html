The details

Three ways to be part of it. The focus is attendance, so there’s no pressure on anyone’s finances.

The Lime

$100 per person

Sunday 15 November 2026, 2pm to 7pm, on the QRC Grounds. Bring your gear, your family, your cooler and your alco. There’s food and activities for everyone, and the obligatory sweat. A dish to share is always welcome.

Menu
KFC and pizza
Drinks
Alcohol is BYOB. Carib, Stag, juice, Coke, ginger ale, LLB and water are provided.
Activities
Football, All 4’s and Uno

The jersey

$200 each

We’re bringing back the nostalgic football kit so everyone can get one of their own. Royal blue with gold trim, QRC and the college crest on the front, Class of ’06 across the back. See the full design below.

Jerseys must be paid for by 27 October so they can be ordered on the 28th.

Pickup details are coming soon.

The project

Any amount you choose

We’re proposing a charitable project to provide much-needed light bulbs for the North and West blocks. The total cost is about $7,500, so we’re asking $200 per person. If you can afford to give more, it will be much appreciated.
